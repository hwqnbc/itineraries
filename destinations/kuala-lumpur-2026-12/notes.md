## Research & options
- **Shortlist (from you):** Aquaria KLCC, Petrosains Discovery Centre, Space & Time Cube (Lot 10, Bukit Bintang), Farm In The City, Monkeys Canopy Resort (Sungai Long, Cheras). **Not going:** Genting, Sunway Lagoon.
- **Drive vs ETS:** the shortlist is reachable by Grab or train, so a car is only needed for Paya Indah (Grab back is unreliable there). It also makes Monkeys Canopy simpler. The comparison is on the trip page, under *Getting there*.
- **Grab rule for this trip:** a place is fine without a car as long as a Grab back to the hotel is easy to book. That's true in the city and suburbs; not at Paya Indah, and worth checking at the Monkeys Canopy hilltop resort {verify}.
- **Monkeys Canopy Resort** (monkeyscanopy.com), Sungai Long, Cheras: Monkeys Splash Zone (indoor water park), Dino Desert, Enchanted Forest, Safari Escape Playland, Conquer indoor extreme park, and a go-kart circuit. One listing shows about RM38 per child and RM50 per adult, with a 2+2 family deal {verify}, but check what it includes. You can also stay overnight at the resort.
- **Space & Time Cube:** Lot 10 Shopping Centre, Bukit Bintang. It's an immersive, naked-eye 3D digital art space with themed zones; allow 1–2 hours. It opens daily, 10am–10pm {verify}.
- **Where to stay if driving:** Bangsar South or Mid Valley (hotel car parks, highway access, 10–20 minutes to KLCC). Avoid KLCC and Bukit Bintang with a car.
- **Singapore car vs JB rental:** a Singapore car needs a VEP and Malaysian insurance, and brings the 3/4-tank fuel rule into play when leaving Singapore {verify}. Renting in JB avoids all of these.
- **RTS Link:** if it's open by December (target end-2026 {verify}), it makes both options' Causeway crossing easier.
