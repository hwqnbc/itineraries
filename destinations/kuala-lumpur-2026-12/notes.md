## Research & options
- **Shortlist (from you):** Aquaria KLCC, Petrosains Discovery Centre, Space and Time Cube, Farm In The City. **Not going:** Genting, Sunway Lagoon.
- **Drive vs ETS:** the shortlist is all reachable by Grab or train, so a car is only needed for FRIM and especially Paya Indah (Grab back is unreliable there). The comparison is on the trip page, under *Getting there*.
- **Grab rule for this trip:** a place is fine without a car as long as a Grab back to the hotel is easy to book. That's true in the city and suburbs; not at Paya Indah, and worth checking at FRIM {verify}.
- **"Monkey canopy":** assumed to be the **FRIM Canopy Walkway** (Kepong). The **KL Forest Eco Park** canopy walk in the city is the no-car alternative. To confirm.
- **Space and Time Cube:** location, opening hours and age suitability to confirm; add it to the map (pois.js) once known.
- **Where to stay if driving:** Bangsar South or Mid Valley (hotel car parks, highway access, 10–20 minutes to KLCC). Avoid KLCC and Bukit Bintang with a car.
- **Singapore car vs JB rental:** a Singapore car needs a VEP and Malaysian insurance, and brings the 3/4-tank fuel rule into play when leaving Singapore {verify}. Renting in JB avoids all of these.
- **RTS Link:** if it's open by December (target end-2026 {verify}), it makes both options' Causeway crossing easier.
