## Research & options
- **Drive vs ETS:** The comparison table is on the trip page, under *Getting there*.
  - **Driving wins** for Genting, Farm In The City and the car-only places: Deerland, the Kuala Selangor fireflies, Legoland on the way home.
  - **ETS wins** for a relaxed journey and a city-only trip.
  - The deciding factors are how much the driver minds KL traffic, and whether the RTS Link is open by then.
- **Where to stay if driving:** Sunway (next to Sunway Lagoon, near highways) vs Mont Kiara or Bangsar South (quieter, but further from Sunway Lagoon). Avoid KLCC and Bukit Bintang with a car: congestion and paid parking.
- **Singapore car vs JB rental:** a Singapore car needs a VEP and Malaysian insurance, and brings the 3/4-tank fuel rule into play when leaving Singapore {verify}. Renting in JB avoids all of these.
- **RTS Link:** if it's open by December (target end-2026 {verify}), it makes both options' Causeway crossing easier.
