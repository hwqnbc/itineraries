A short overland trip from Singapore: a theme-and-water park with animals to feed, a petting farm, a mountain-top theme park and KL's bird park and aquarium. The big choice is **how to get there**: drive from JB, or take the ETS train. This draft assumes **driving**, because a car reaches places the trains don't. The ETS version is in the comparison below.

> Idea stage. Anything marked {verify} (opening times, feeding sessions, train times, entry rules) must be checked before booking. December is Singapore's school holiday, so expect long Causeway queues.

## 👪 Who's going {#whos-going}
- Family of three: two adults and one boy aged 8.
- What he loves: feeding and petting animals, and theme parks.
- Plan rules: at most 2 big activities a day, a midday break, and the long drives kept to their own days (Days 1 and 6).

## 🚗 Getting there: drive or ETS? {#flights}
No flights: this trip goes overland.

| | 🚗 Drive (rent a car in JB) | 🚆 ETS train (JB Sentral → KL Sentral) |
|---|---|---|
| Journey | About 350 km on the North–South Expressway, 3.5–4.5 hours without jams {verify} | About 4.5–5 hours {verify}, plus getting to JB Sentral |
| Causeway | You cross by bus or taxi and collect the rental car in JB. Renting a Malaysian car avoids the VEP needed for Singapore cars {verify} | Same crossing, by bus or taxi to JB Sentral. The RTS Link (Woodlands ↔ Bukit Chagar) is due around end-2026 {verify} and would make this much easier |
| In KL | Door-to-door, and the car carries the luggage. But KL rush-hour traffic is bad, and city-centre hotels charge for parking | Grab and LRT/MRT. No parking or traffic stress for the driver |
| Places | Reaches the car-only places: Deerland, the Kuala Selangor fireflies and monkeys, a Melaka stop, and Legoland Malaysia on the way home | City sights and Sunway Lagoon by Grab. Genting by bus or Grab; the car-only places are hard |
| Hotel | Stay **outside the city centre** with free or easy parking and fast highway access (e.g. Sunway) | Stay near **KL Sentral** (ETS arrives there) or KLCC |
| Best if | You want Genting, Farm In The City and the car-only extras | You want a relaxing ride and a city-focused trip |

**Recommendation:** drive, and base at **Sunway**. Sunway Lagoon is next to the hotels, the highways are close, and hotel parking is simpler than in the city centre {verify}. For the KL city day, leave the car at the hotel and take Grab. If you choose ETS instead, swap the base to KL Sentral and use Grab for Days 2–4 (see *ETS version* under the day plan).

## 🏨 Accommodation {#accommodation}
**If driving, parking and traffic decide where to stay.** KLCC and Bukit Bintang hotels have heavy traffic around them, paid valet or parking, and tight car-park ramps {verify}. A base next to a highway, with a hotel car park, saves time every day.

## 🗓️ Day-by-day {#days}

### Day 1 · Singapore → JB → drive to KL 🧳
- **Luggage:** Carry it across the Causeway (bus or taxi) to the car-rental pickup in JB; after that the bags stay in the car until the hotel.
- **Morning:** Cross early (before 7am), because December weekends have long queues {verify}. Collect the rental car in JB and buy or load a Touch 'n Go card for tolls.
- **Midday:** Break halfway for lunch, either at a highway rest stop or a short detour into [Melaka](map:Jonker+Street+Melaka) {verify}.
- **Afternoon:** Check in at Sunway, then pool time. Arrive before 5pm to miss the evening rush.

### Day 2 · Sunway Lagoon 🎢
- **All day:** [Sunway Lagoon](map:Sunway+Lagoon), walkable from the hotel so there's no driving. It has water-park slides, theme-park rides, and a **Wildlife Park** with animal feeding and encounters {verify}.
- **Midday:** Go back to the hotel to rest and have lunch, then return in the afternoon (check whether your ticket allows re-entry {verify}).

### Day 3 · Farm In The City 🐐
- **Morning:** Drive about 20–30 minutes to [Farm In The City](map:Farm+In+The+City+Seri+Kembangan) in Seri Kembangan. It's a hands-on petting and feeding farm, great for an 8-year-old {verify}. Go at opening, before it gets hot.
- **Afternoon:** Rest at the hotel and pool.
- **Evening:** Mall dinner at Sunway Pyramid, still walking distance.

### Day 4 · Genting SkyWorlds 🎡
- **Morning:** Drive about 1–1.5 hours to Genting Highlands {verify}. Leave by 8am to beat traffic, park at the [Awana SkyWay](map:Awana+SkyWay+Genting) station, and take the cable car up.
- **Day:** [Genting SkyWorlds](map:Genting+SkyWorlds+Theme+Park) outdoor theme park. It's cool up there (about 16–23 °C {verify}), so bring a jacket; the indoor park works as a rain backup.
- **Evening:** Drive back down before dark, avoiding the Sunday-evening jam if you can.

### Day 5 · KL city: Bird Park & Aquaria 🦜
- **Getting around:** Leave the car at the hotel. Grab into the city avoids city parking and the rush hour.
- **Morning:** [KL Bird Park](map:KL+Bird+Park), one of the world's largest free-flight aviaries, with feeding sessions {verify}.
- **Afternoon:** [Aquaria KLCC](map:Aquaria+KLCC) for the underwater tunnel and feeding times {verify}. If it rains, go to [Petrosains](map:Petrosains+KLCC) science centre next door.
- **Evening:** KLCC Park playground and the fountain show at dusk, then Grab back.

### Day 6 · Drive home 🧳
- **Luggage:** Check out; the bags ride in the car back to JB.
- **Morning:** Leave after breakfast, but after the morning rush (about 9:30am).
- **Afternoon:** Return the car in JB, then cross back. Weekday afternoons are usually quieter than Sunday evenings {verify}.

### Swap-in options
- **[Deerland Park](map:Deerland+Park+Lanchang)** (car only, about 1.5 hours east): feed and pet deer and other gentle animals {verify}. Could replace Day 3.
- **[Zoo Negara](map:Zoo+Negara)**: the national zoo, with pandas {verify}. On KL's edge, easiest by car.
- **[Kuala Selangor](map:Bukit+Malawati+Kuala+Selangor)** (car only, about 1.5 hours): silvered leaf monkeys on Bukit Malawati in the late afternoon, then a **firefly** boat ride after dark {verify}. A late evening.
- **[Batu Caves](map:Batu+Caves)**: colourful steps and cheeky monkeys (hold on to your snacks). Avoid Thaipusam.
- **[Legoland Malaysia](map:Legoland+Malaysia)** in JB (car only): add a night in JB on the way home, rather than squeezing it into Day 6.

### ETS version
- **Day 1 🧳:** Bus or taxi across the Causeway to JB Sentral, then the ETS to KL Sentral, then walk or Grab to a KL Sentral hotel. **Luggage:** it's with you on the train; trains take suitcases {verify}.
- **Days 2–3:** Sunway Lagoon and Farm In The City by Grab (about 30–45 minutes each way, longer in rush hour).
- **Day 4:** Genting by the express bus from KL Sentral or by Grab, then the SkyWay {verify}.
- **Day 5:** Bird Park and Aquaria by LRT/MRT or Grab, which is easier from a city base.
- **Day 6 🧳:** ETS back to JB Sentral, then cross home.
- The car-only swap-ins (Deerland, Kuala Selangor) need a day car or driver hired in KL.

## ℹ️ Practical info {#practical}
- **Weather:** December is warm (about 24–32 °C) with afternoon thunderstorms {verify}. Plan outdoor things for the morning. Genting is much cooler.
- **Crowds:** Singapore and Malaysian school holidays: the Causeway, Sunway Lagoon and Genting are busiest at weekends and around Christmas and New Year.
- **Traffic in KL:** avoid driving at about 7–9:30am and 5–8pm on weekdays. Friday evenings and rain make it worse. Use Waze or Google Maps traffic.
- **Driving:** left-hand traffic, like Singapore. Tolls on the expressways use Touch 'n Go or RFID {verify}. Check whether the rental company lets you cross into Singapore (usually not).
- **Money:** Malaysian Ringgit (MYR). Cards and e-wallets are widely accepted. Touch 'n Go also pays for parking.
- **Power plugs:** Type G, 240 V, the same as Singapore. No adapter needed.
- **Entry:** Singapore passports are visa-free; check the digital arrival card rules {verify}.
