The **car trip**: rent a car in JB and base in **Putrajaya**, south of KL, for the places a train and Grab can't reach easily. It **never enters the KL city centre**: the city sights (Aquaria, Petrosains, the Planetarium) are on the **[ETS city trip](../kuala-lumpur-2026-12-ets/trip.md)** on 1–9 Dec. This one covers **Paya Indah Wetlands** (hippos), **Monkeys Canopy** (with its indoor water park), a whole day at **GAMEON The Mines** (indoor), and **Putrajaya**, then a straight drive home with food stops in Seremban and Yong Peng.

> Planning. Dates chosen to stay off the roads around Christmas: up on Sun 20, home on Sat 26, with the last night in JB so the car goes back the next morning. Anything marked {verify} must be checked before booking. It's the school holidays in both countries, so expect crowds and peak prices; the attraction days are all weekdays.

## 👪 Who's going {#whos-going}
- Family of three: two adults and one boy aged 8.
- What he loves: feeding and petting animals, and theme parks.
- Plan rules: at most 2 big activities a day, a midday break, and the long drives (Days 1 and 6) kept to themselves. Theme parks get the **whole day** (Days 3 and 4), with nothing else planned: he'll be tired afterwards.

## 🚗 Getting there: drive from JB {#flights}
1. **Cross the Causeway** to JB early (about 7am) by bus or taxi (or the RTS Link if open {verify}), and collect a **JB rental car**. A Malaysian car avoids the VEP, the Malaysian insurance and the 3/4-tank rule that apply to a Singapore car {verify}.
2. **Drive the North–South Expressway to Putrajaya**, about 3–3.5 hours without jams {verify}. Tolls use Touch 'n Go or RFID {verify}. Putrajaya is just off the expressway, so you never drive through KL.
3. **Coming back:** drive to JB on **Fri 25**, stay **one night in JB**, and **return the car on Sat 26 morning**. No rush to beat a 6pm return time after a long drive.

## 🗓️ Day-by-day {#days}

### Day 1 · Sun 20 Dec · Singapore → JB → drive to Putrajaya 🧳
- **Luggage:** Carry it across the Causeway to the rental pickup in JB; after that it stays in the car.
- **Morning:** Cross early (about 7am): it's a school-holiday weekend {verify}. Collect the car and load a Touch 'n Go card.
- **Midday:** Lunch at a highway rest stop (R&R) on the way {verify}.
- **Afternoon:** Check in at Putrajaya by about 3–4pm, then pool time.
- **Evening:** An easy walk by the lake to see Putrajaya's lit bridges and buildings after dark {verify}; dinner nearby.

### Day 2 · Mon 21 Dec · Paya Indah Wetlands & Putra Mosque 🦛🕌
- **Why Monday:** Paya Indah is open on Mondays and normally closes on Tuesdays {verify}; Monkeys Canopy's Splash Zone, which closes on Mondays, is on Tuesday.
- **Early morning (8am):** [Paya Indah Wetlands](map:Paya+Indah+Wetlands+Dengkil) (⏱ 2–2.5 h), Dengkil, about 15–20 minutes from Putrajaya. Lakes, birds, crocodiles and **hippos**; it opens at 8am (you checked), so go first thing while it's cool. Check the feeding times {verify}.
- **Late morning:** Back to Putrajaya (about 15–20 minutes) and lunch.
- **Then:** [Putra Mosque](map:Putra+Mosque+Putrajaya) (⏱ 30–45 min), the pink mosque: free; dress modestly (robes lent at the entrance) {verify}. Fine on a Monday; on **Fridays** it's closed to visitors until mid-afternoon {verify}.
- **And:** [Cruise Tasik Putrajaya](map:Cruise+Tasik+Putrajaya) (⏱ 45 min) from the jetty by Putra Mosque: about RM50 per adult and RM35 per child, or a cheaper 25-minute cruise at 10am and 7pm {verify}.
- **Afternoon:** Rest and pool at the hotel.
- **If it's stormy:** swap the cruise for **IOI City Mall / District 21** (indoor, see swap-ins).

### Day 3 · Tue 22 Dec · Monkeys Canopy Resort 🦕
- **Getting there:** Drive about 30–40 minutes north-east to Sungai Long, leaving about 9:15–9:30am to arrive around the 10am opening {verify}. The resort has parking {verify}.
- **All day:** [Monkeys Canopy Resort](map:Monkeys+Canopy+Resort+Sungai+Long) (⏱ 4–6 h), a hilltop resort with several parks: **Monkeys Splash Zone** (indoor water park), **Dino Desert**, **Enchanted Forest**, **Safari Escape Playland** and the **Conquer** indoor extreme park {verify}. Check which parks the ticket covers, and the height limits {verify}.
- **Not on a Monday:** **Monkeys Splash Zone** is **closed on Mondays for maintenance** (the next day instead if the Monday is a public holiday) {verify}. Tue–Sun about 10am–8pm {verify}. Splash Zone tickets were about RM65 per adult and RM45 per child when it opened in Jan 2025 {verify}.
- **Re-entry:** not yet confirmed {verify}. Until then, plan to stay in: lunch at the resort.
- **Outside food:** check whether you may bring your own food and drinks {verify}. If not, eat a proper breakfast first and budget for meals inside.
- **Getting back:** Stay until closing (about 8pm), then drive back to Putrajaya, about 35–40 minutes once the evening rush has eased {verify}.

### Day 4 · Wed 23 Dec · GAMEON Themepark, The Mines (whole day) 🎮
- **Getting there:** Drive about 15–20 minutes north to The Mines Shopping Mall, Seri Kembangan, leaving about 9:30am; mall parking {verify}.
- **All day (10am until he's done):** [GAMEON Themepark, The Mines](map:GAMEON+Themepark+The+Mines+Seri+Kembangan) (⏱ whole day), a big 3-storey indoor play park: Imagination Playland, a **Splash Lagoon** water-play area, bowling, Floor Is Lava, an inflatable challenge, wall climbing, ninja challenges and sports games {verify}. One listing shows **unlimited play for about RM34**; open 10am–10pm daily {verify}. Indoors, so storms don't matter. Nothing else planned today.
- **Re-entry:** not found {verify}; check whether the wristband allows leaving for lunch in the mall.
- **Outside food:** not found {verify}; plan meals at the mall's food outlets.
- **Socks:** check whether grip socks are required {verify}.
- **Getting back:** About 15–20 minutes to Putrajaya; after 8pm the roads are clear.

### Day 5 · Thu 24 Dec · Putrajaya, relaxed (Christmas Eve) 🚲
- **Morning (8am):** [Taman Botani Putrajaya](map:Taman+Botani+Putrajaya) (⏱ 1–2 h): **rent bikes** for the flat lakeside paths, from about RM10 an hour; twin bikes about RM20 per 30 minutes {verify}. Hours vary by source; it may be closed on Tuesdays {verify}. No bikes on the canopy bridge. Ride early, before it's hot.
- **Late morning:** Back to the hotel to cool off; the Putra Mosque and the lake cruise are on Day 2.
- **Afternoon:** Rest and pool at the hotel, or **IOI City Mall** (shopping and District 21; see swap-ins) early in the afternoon, before the Christmas Eve crowds, if he has energy left.
- **Evening:** Christmas Eve dinner; malls will be busy {verify}.

### Day 6 · Fri 25 Dec · Drive to JB 🧳
- **Luggage:** Check out; the bags ride in the car to JB.
- **Morning:** Leave Putrajaya about 10am; about 3–3.5 hours to JB on the North–South Expressway {verify}.
- **Snack stop (about 10:40):** [Empayar Seremban Siew Pow](map:Empayar+Seremban+Siew+Pow) (⏱ 20–30 min), near the Seremban exit (218): Seremban's famous baked char siew buns, to take away for the car {verify}.
- **Lunch (about 12:30):** [Kluang RailCoffee, Yong Peng](map:Kluang+RailCoffee+Yong+Peng) (⏱ 45–60 min), about a minute from the Yong Peng exit (241): the Kluang railway kopitiam's kaya-butter toast, Hainanese coffee and local dishes {verify}.
- **Christmas Day:** a public holiday: check both places' opening hours {verify}.
- **Friday:** prayers around 12:15–2:45pm; some small stalls at rest stops may close for a while, but the kopitiam and the R&R food courts should be fine {verify}.
- **Afternoon:** Reach JB about 2–2:30pm; check in at the JB hotel.
- **Evening:** Dinner in JB.

### Day 7 · Sat 26 Dec · Return the car → home 🧳
- **Luggage:** From the JB hotel to the car-return office, then across the Causeway on foot, bus or taxi.
- **Morning:** **Return the car** (check the rental office's Saturday hours) {verify}, then cross the Causeway before the afternoon crowds. Most Singapore travellers stay in Malaysia until Sunday, so Saturday morning should be calmer heading home {verify}.

### Swap-in options
- **[IOI City Mall](map:IOI+City+Mall+Putrajaya) (⏱ 2–4 h) with [District 21](map:District+21+IOI+City+Mall) (⏱ 2–3 h): the nearest rainy-day option.** One of Malaysia's biggest malls, about 10–15 minutes from Putrajaya, with a big car park {verify}; easy for a dinner on any evening. **District 21** (Level 1) is an indoor adventure park with 11 challenges: a giant tube slide, ropes course, go-pedal track, maze and climbing {verify}. Weekdays 12–8pm, weekends and public holidays 10am–8pm, last tickets 5:30pm; about RM64 weekdays / RM80 weekends and holidays, RM10 for a watching adult {verify}. **👟 Shoes: everyone playing must wear closed shoes (no sandals or slippers); bring socks too** {verify}. The mall also has the **Icescape** Olympic-size ice rink (gloves and socks needed; penguin skating aids for hire) {verify}. Fits the free Thu 24 afternoon (Day 5) or a stormy Day 2. It's a similar idea to GAMEON (Day 4), so mainly for the mall and shopping.
- **[A'Famosa Safari Wonderland](map:A+Famosa+Safari+Wonderland)** (⏱ full day), Alor Gajah, Melaka: a **truck drive through a safari park**, walk-through areas and animal shows, with an evening carnival and fireworks {verify}. About 30 minutes off the expressway {verify}. Could break the Day 6 drive, reaching JB in the evening.
- **Kuala Selangor** (afternoon and evening; about 1.5 hours each way from Putrajaya {verify}): [Bukit Melawati](map:Bukit+Melawati+Kuala+Selangor) (⏱ 1–1.5 h) for the **silver leaf monkeys** in the late afternoon, a seafood dinner, then a **firefly boat** at [Kampung Kuantan](map:Kampung+Kuantan+Firefly+Park) (⏱ 30–45 min) after dark, best 8–11pm and away from the full moon {verify}. About RM100 per boat of four for foreigners in one listing {verify}; book ahead at holidays. Back about 11pm, so it's a late night.

## ℹ️ Practical info {#practical}
- **Dates & prices:** **Sun 20 – Sat 26 Dec 2026**, in the Malaysian and Singapore school holidays: expect crowds and peak prices. The attraction days (Mon 21 – Thu 24) are weekdays, before Christmas. You stay put on Christmas Eve, and the long drive is on Christmas Day itself, when the roads are holiday-quiet {verify}. Christmas (Fri 25) is a public holiday in both countries.
- **Friday prayers:** the only Friday is Day 6, a driving day, so it's barely affected. See the [Malaysia guide](../../docs/countries/malaysia.md).
- **Weather:** December is warm (about 24–32 °C) with afternoon thunderstorms {verify}. Outdoor places (Paya Indah, the bikes) go in the morning or as soon as they open.
- **Traffic:** from Putrajaya most drives go away from KL; see [Rush-hour direction](#accommodation). Use Waze or Google Maps traffic.
- **Parking:** hotel, mall and attraction car parks mostly take Touch 'n Go or card {verify}.
- **Driving:** left-hand traffic, like Singapore. Check whether the rental lets you drive into Singapore (usually not).
- **Power plugs:** Type G, 240 V, the same as Singapore. No adapter needed.
- **Entry:** Singapore passports are visa-free; check the digital arrival card rules {verify}.
