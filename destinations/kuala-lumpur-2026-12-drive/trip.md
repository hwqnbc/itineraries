The **car trip**: rent a car in JB and base in **Putrajaya**, south of KL, for the places a train and Grab can't reach easily. It **never enters the KL city centre**: the city sights (Aquaria, Petrosains, the Planetarium) are on the **[ETS city trip](../kuala-lumpur-2026-12-ets/trip.md)** on 1–9 Dec. This one covers **Paya Indah Wetlands** (hippos) and **Farm In The City**, **Monkeys Canopy** (with its indoor water park), a whole day at **GAMEON The Mines** (indoor), **Putrajaya** (bikes, deer, horses), a free Christmas Day, then the drive south with food stops in Seremban and Yong Peng and **two nights in JB** (Eco Botanic, then JB City Square).

> Planning. Up on Sun 20; drive south on **Sat 26**; two nights in JB; **home on Mon 28**, avoiding the Sunday jam at the end of the Christmas long weekend. The last night is near the car-return office, so the car goes back the next morning. Anything marked {verify} must be checked before booking. It's the school holidays in both countries, so expect crowds and peak prices; the attraction days are all weekdays.

## 👪 Who's going {#whos-going}
- Family of three: two adults and one boy aged 8.
- What he loves: feeding and petting animals, and theme parks.
- Plan rules: at most 2 big activities a day, a midday break, and the long drives (Days 1 and 7) kept light. Theme parks get the **whole day** (Days 3 and 4), with nothing else planned: he'll be tired afterwards.

## 🚗 Getting there: drive from JB {#flights}
1. **Cross the Causeway** to JB early (about 7am) by bus or taxi (or the RTS Link if open {verify}), and collect a **JB rental car**. A Malaysian car avoids the VEP, the Malaysian insurance and the 3/4-tank rule that apply to a Singapore car {verify}.
2. **Drive the North–South Expressway to Putrajaya**, about 3–3.5 hours without jams {verify}. Tolls use Touch 'n Go or RFID {verify}. Putrajaya is just off the expressway, so you never drive through KL.
3. **Coming back:** drive to JB on **Sat 26**, a night at **Eco Botanic** (Iskandar Puteri), a night near **JB City Square** (the rental office), and **return the car on Mon 28 morning**. That avoids the Sunday-evening Causeway jam at the end of the Christmas long weekend, and there's no rush to beat a 6pm return.

## 🗓️ Day-by-day {#days}

### Day 1 · Sun 20 Dec · Singapore → JB → drive to Putrajaya 🧳
- **Luggage:** Carry it across the Causeway to the rental pickup in JB; after that it stays in the car.
- **Morning:** Cross early (about 7am): it's a school-holiday weekend {verify}. Collect the car and load a Touch 'n Go card.
- **Midday:** Lunch at a highway rest stop (R&R) on the way {verify}.
- **Afternoon:** Check in at Putrajaya by about 3–4pm, then rest (pool if the hotel has one).
- **Evening:** An easy walk by the lake to see Putrajaya's lit bridges and buildings after dark {verify}; dinner nearby.

### Day 2 · Mon 21 Dec · Paya Indah Wetlands & Farm In The City 🦛🐐
- **Why Monday:** Paya Indah is open on Mondays and normally closes on Tuesdays {verify}; Monkeys Canopy's Splash Zone, which closes on Mondays, is on Tuesday.
- **Morning (8am until about noon):** [Paya Indah Wetlands](map:Paya+Indah+Wetlands+Dengkil) (⏱ 3–4 h, relaxed), Dengkil, about 15–20 minutes from Putrajaya. Lakes, birds, crocodiles and **hippos**; it opens at 8am (you checked), so go first thing while it's cool and take it slowly. Check the feeding times {verify}.
- **Lunch (about 12–1:30):** on the way, around Seri Kembangan or The Mines.
- **Afternoon (about 1:30–4):** [Farm In The City](map:Farm+In+The+City+Seri+Kembangan) (⏱ 2–3 h), Seri Kembangan, about 25–30 minutes from Paya Indah: a hands-on petting and feeding farm {verify}. Listings show about RM58 adult / RM48 child for foreigners; check the [one-day pass page](https://farminthecity.my/ticket-packages-one-day-pass/) {verify}. **Arrive by about 2pm** and check the closing and last-entry times {verify}. School holidays, so it's busy, but it's spread out. Parts are covered if the 3–6pm storms come early {verify}.
- **If he's tired:** skip the farm and rest; two gentle animal places are within his limit, but Paya Indah is a long morning.
- **Getting back:** about 15–20 minutes to Putrajaya; rest at the hotel.

### Day 3 · Tue 22 Dec · Monkeys Canopy Resort 🦕
- **Getting there:** Drive about 30–40 minutes north-east to Sungai Long, leaving about 9:15–9:30am to arrive around the 10am opening {verify}. The resort has parking {verify}.
- **All day:** [Monkeys Canopy Resort](map:Monkeys+Canopy+Resort+Sungai+Long) (⏱ 4–6 h), a hilltop resort with several parks: **Monkeys Splash Zone** (indoor water park), **Dino Desert**, **Enchanted Forest**, **Safari Escape Playland** and the **Conquer** indoor extreme park {verify}. Check which parks the ticket covers, and the height limits {verify}.
- **Not on a Monday:** **Monkeys Splash Zone** is **closed on Mondays for maintenance** (the next day instead if the Monday is a public holiday) {verify}. Tue–Sun about 10am–8pm {verify}. Splash Zone tickets were about RM65 per adult and RM45 per child when it opened in Jan 2025 {verify}.
- **Re-entry:** not yet confirmed {verify}. Until then, plan to stay in: lunch at the resort.
- **Outside food:** check whether you may bring your own food and drinks {verify}. If not, eat a proper breakfast first and budget for meals inside.
- **Getting back:** Stay until closing (about 8pm), then drive back to Putrajaya, about 35–40 minutes once the evening rush has eased {verify}.

### Day 4 · Wed 23 Dec · GAMEON Themepark, The Mines (whole day) 🎮
- **Getting there:** Drive about 15–20 minutes north to The Mines Shopping Mall, Seri Kembangan, leaving about 9:30am; mall parking {verify}.
- **All day (10am until he's done):** [GAMEON Themepark, The Mines](map:GAMEON+Themepark+The+Mines+Seri+Kembangan) (⏱ whole day), a big 3-storey indoor play park: Imagination Playland, a **Splash Lagoon** water-play area, bowling, Floor Is Lava, an inflatable challenge, wall climbing, ninja challenges and sports games {verify}. One listing shows **unlimited play for about RM34**; open 10am–10pm daily {verify}. Indoors, so storms don't matter. Nothing else planned today.
- **Re-entry:** not found {verify}; check whether the wristband allows leaving for lunch in the mall.
- **Outside food:** not found {verify}; plan meals at the mall's food outlets.
- **Socks:** check whether grip socks are required {verify}.
- **Getting back:** About 15–20 minutes to Putrajaya; after 8pm the roads are clear.

### Day 5 · Thu 24 Dec · Putrajaya: bikes, deer & Putra Mosque (Christmas Eve) 🚲🦌🕌
- **Morning (8am):** [Taman Botani Putrajaya](map:Taman+Botani+Putrajaya) (⏱ 1–2 h): **rent bikes** for the flat lakeside paths, from about RM10 an hour; twin bikes about RM20 per 30 minutes {verify}. Hours vary by source; it may be closed on Tuesdays {verify}. No bikes on the canopy bridge. Ride early, before it's hot.
- **Breakfast (about 9:30):** [The Glasshopper](map:The+Glasshopper+Putrajaya) (⏱ 45–60 min), right next to Taman Botani: brunch-style menu (shakshouka, burgers), coffee that reviewers rate highly; order at the counter even when seated upstairs; free, easy parking. Open daily 8:30am–7pm (Google Maps, Oct 2026).
- **Deer park (about 10:30):** [Laman Rusa @ Taman Botani](map:Laman+Rusa+Taman+Botani+Putrajaya) (⏱ 30–45 min), a short walk from The Glasshopper: a fenced walkway through the deer enclosure to see them up close. **Free**, open **7am–7pm daily** (Places Malaysia, Nov 2025) {verify}. Feeding isn't mentioned: check on the day.
- **Late morning:** [Putra Mosque](map:Putra+Mosque+Putrajaya) (⏱ 30–45 min), the pink mosque, a few minutes away: free; dress modestly (robes lent at the entrance) {verify}. Fine on a Thursday; on **Fridays** it's closed to visitors until mid-afternoon {verify}.
- **Then:** [Cruise Tasik Putrajaya](map:Cruise+Tasik+Putrajaya) (⏱ 45 min) from the jetty by Putra Mosque: about RM50 per adult and RM35 per child, or a cheaper 25-minute cruise at 10am and 7pm {verify}.
- **Afternoon:** Lunch, then rest at the hotel (pool if there is one).
- **Evening:** Christmas Eve dinner; malls will be busy {verify}.

### Day 6 · Fri 25 Dec · Christmas Day: free (swap-ins below) 🎄
- **Why free:** a public holiday, so crowds everywhere, and a **Friday** (prayers around 12:15–2:45pm, when some places pause) {verify}. A day to rest, or pick one of the **Christmas Day options** in the swap-ins.
- **Easy default:** a slow morning, rest at the hotel, then **IOI City Mall** in the evening for the Christmas decorations and dinner {verify}.

### Day 7 · Sat 26 Dec · Ekues breakfast → drive to JB (Eco Botanic) 🧳🐴
- **Luggage:** Check out; the bags ride in the car to JB (keep them out of sight while you eat).
- **Breakfast (8am):** [Ekues Cabin Cafe](map:Ekues+Cabin+Cafe+Putrajaya) (⏱ 1–1.5 h), an open-air café in the **Putrajaya Equestrian Park**, south Putrajaya, towards the expressway: breakfast while **watching the horses**. Open 8am–7pm on Saturdays (closed Mondays; Fridays 8am–1pm and 3–7pm) (Google Maps, Oct 2026). Reviews say it's a bit pricey and service can be slow. Optional **pony/horse joy ride**, about RM15 adult / RM10 child: check the ride times {verify}.
- **Drive (about 9:45):** about 3.5–4 hours to Eco Botanic on the North–South Expressway {verify}. Boxing Day: most holiday traffic is still heading north {verify}.
- **Snack stop (about 10:30–11):** [Empayar Seremban Siew Pow](map:Empayar+Seremban+Siew+Pow) (⏱ 20–30 min), near the Seremban exit (218): Seremban's famous baked char siew buns, to take away for the car {verify}.
- **Lunch (about 12:30–1):** [Kluang RailCoffee, Yong Peng](map:Kluang+RailCoffee+Yong+Peng) (⏱ 45–60 min), about a minute from the Yong Peng exit (241): kaya-butter toast, Hainanese coffee and local dishes {verify}.
- **Afternoon (about 2:30):** Check in at **Eco Botanic**, Iskandar Puteri. Then [Corgi's Talk](map:Corgi%27s+Talk+Eco+Botanic) (⏱ 1 h), a dog café with three resident corgis {verify}, or [Mall of Medini](map:Mall+of+Medini) (⏱ 1–2 h) if it's raining {verify}.
- **Dinner:** [Restoran Hua Mui, Eco Botanic](map:Restoran+Hua+Mui+Eco+Botanic) (⏱ 1 h), the Eco Botanic branch of JB's original **Hainanese chicken chop** kopitiam {verify}; or the retro-style **Ah Po Jia** café {verify}.

### Day 8 · Sun 27 Dec · JB city: cafés & heritage streets 🧳☕
- **Luggage:** Check out of Eco Botanic; the bags ride to the City Square hotel.
- **Morning:** A slow breakfast in Eco Botanic, then drive about 25–35 minutes to **JB City Square** and check in or drop the bags. **Move before about 1pm**: on Sunday afternoon Singapore-bound cars queue around the Causeway, right by City Square {verify}. Then leave the car at the hotel.
- **Afternoon (on foot):** [Jalan Dhoby & Jalan Tan Hiok Nee](map:Jalan+Dhoby+Johor+Bahru) (⏱ 2–3 h), about 10 minutes' walk from City Square: JB's heritage streets, full of **cafés** (Roost Café and others), with **[Cult Classics](map:Cult+Classics+Jalan+Dhoby)**, a vintage photobooth studio with themed rooms {verify}. New: **Slowspace** (specialty coffee, Jalan Ibrahim) {verify}.
- **Animal café:** [DuDu Duck Cafe](map:DuDu+Duck+Cafe+R%26F+Princess+Cove) (⏱ 1 h) at R&F Princess Cove, near the Causeway: a new café with **resident ducks** (opened 2026) {verify}. Or **Capyba Café**, where you can meet **capybaras**: check its location and booking {verify}.
- **Evening:** Dinner near City Square; stay on foot, as the roads stay jammed until late on Sundays {verify}.

### Day 9 · Mon 28 Dec · Return the car → home 🧳
- **Luggage:** From the hotel to the car-return office, then across the Causeway on foot, bus or taxi.
- **Morning:** **Return the car about 9am** (check the rental office's Monday hours) {verify}, then cross the Causeway about **10–11am**, after the 5–9am commuter rush into Singapore. The long-weekend crowd went home on Sunday {verify}.

Weekdays 12–8pm, weekends and public holidays 10am–8pm, last tickets 5:30pm; about RM64 weekdays / RM80 weekends and holidays, RM10 for a watching adult {verify}. **👟 Shoes: everyone playing must wear closed shoes (no sandals or slippers); bring socks too** {verify}. The mall also has the **Icescape** Olympic-size ice rink (gloves and socks needed; penguin skating aids for hire) {verify}. On Christmas Day, go at opening; it's busy.

About RM100 per boat of four for foreigners in one listing {verify}; **book ahead** at holidays. Back about 11pm, so Sat 26 starts slowly.

- **Ekues Cabin Cafe and a horse ride** (see Day 7): Friday hours 8am–1pm and 3–7pm, if you'd rather do it on Christmas Day.

## ℹ️ Practical info {#practical}
- **Dates & prices:** **Sun 20 – Mon 28 Dec 2026**, in the Malaysian and Singapore school holidays: expect crowds and peak prices. The attraction days (Mon 21 – Thu 24) are weekdays, before Christmas. Christmas (Fri 25) is a public holiday in both countries and a free day near the base; the drive south is on Sat 26, and you cross home on Mon 28, after the long weekend's Sunday jam {verify}.
- **Friday prayers:** the only Friday is Day 6 (Christmas Day, free): around 12:15–2:45pm, the Putra Mosque is closed to visitors and some places pause (Ekues closes 1–3pm), so plan Friday options for the morning or late afternoon. See the [Malaysia guide](../../docs/countries/malaysia.md).
- **Weather:** December is warm (about 24–32 °C) with afternoon thunderstorms {verify}. Outdoor places (Paya Indah, the bikes) go in the morning or as soon as they open.
- **Traffic:** from Putrajaya most drives go away from KL; see [Rush-hour direction](#accommodation). Use Waze or Google Maps traffic.
- **Parking:** hotel, mall and attraction car parks mostly take Touch 'n Go or card {verify}.
- **Driving:** left-hand traffic, like Singapore. Check whether the rental lets you drive into Singapore (usually not).
- **Power plugs:** Type G, 240 V, the same as Singapore. No adapter needed.
- **Entry:** Singapore passports are visa-free; check the digital arrival card rules {verify}.
