## Trip-specific overrides
- Animal welfare: skip elephant rides, elephant bathing and animal shows at Bali Zoo and Bali Safari Park. Keeper talks, feeding sessions and the orangutan breakfast are fine.

## Research & options
- **Bali or Taipei?** Destination not decided yet. Both are 6-day drafts built for the same family.

  | | Bali (June) | Taipei (June) | Taipei (December) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Weather | Dry season, sunny, 24–31 °C | Hot and humid, plum rains, early typhoons | 15–21 °C, drizzly |
  | Animals | Safari park, orangutan breakfast, farm feeding | Zoo (pandas), calf feeding, aquarium | Same as June, plus strawberry picking |
  | Theme parks | Waterbom, Safari Fun Zone, treetop ropes | Leofoo, Children's Amusement Park | Same; water parks may be closed |
  | Getting around | Car with driver every day (luggage easy) | MRT plus a charter for 2 days | Same |
  | Watch out | School-holiday crowds late Jun–Jul; strong sun | Heat, typhoons | Wet days, New Year crowds |

  Bali suits **June**, and Taiwan suits **December**. See the [Indonesia guide](../../docs/countries/indonesia.md) and the [Taiwan guide](../../docs/countries/taiwan.md).
- **Sanur vs Nusa Dua for the beach part:** Sanur is closer to the safari park and cheaper; Nusa Dua has resort beaches but is further from the Day 2–4 sights.
- **Night Safari:** fun, but a late night; keep it optional and don't book it after Waterbom day.
