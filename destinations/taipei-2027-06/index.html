🇹🇼 Taipei, Taiwan

Planning 📅 June 2027 · exact dates TBD (6-day draft) 👪 Participants: default-family

A kid-first trip built around animals and theme parks: pandas at Taipei Zoo, a gondola ride, a safari theme park, bottle-feeding calves on a farm and an aquarium on the way home.

👪 Who's going

  • Family of three: two adults and one boy aged 8.
  • What he loves: feeding and petting animals, and theme parks.
  • Plan rules: at most 2 big activities a day, a midday break away from the heat, and long drives kept to their own days.

✈️ Flights

Fly into Taoyuan International Airport (TPE). The Airport MRT express goes to Taipei Main Station in about 35–40 minutes . Some regional flights land at Songshan (TSA), which is in the city.

🏨 Accommodation

Base yourselves in central Taipei close to an MRT station. Good areas are around Taipei Main Station (for the Airport MRT), Zhongshan, or Xinyi. Book a family or triple room. Leofoo's on-site resort is worth considering for Day 4 night .

🗓️ Day-by-day

Day 1 · Arrive & settle in
  • Arrive: TPE → Airport MRT → hotel. Buy EasyCards at the airport or a convenience store.
  • Afternoon: Rest and have a quiet look around the hotel's neighbourhood.
  • Evening: If everyone still has energy, try an easy night market: Raohe Street Night Market.
Day 2 · Taipei Zoo & Maokong Gondola 🐼
  • Morning: Taipei Zoo (MRT Brown Line, Taipei Zoo station). Arrive at opening, when the animals are more active and it's cooler. See the giant pandas, the Formosan animals area, penguins, and the children's zoo area.
  • Midday: Lunch and an indoor break (the Panda House and Education Center are indoors).
  • Afternoon: Maokong Gondola from the zoo. Queue for an "Eyes of Maokong" glass-floor cabin. It usually closes on Mondays for maintenance, so don't schedule this day on a Monday .
  • Evening: Tea and snacks at Maokong, then the gondola back down. Early night.
Day 3 · Children's Amusement Park & Science Museum 🎡
  • Morning: Taipei Children's Amusement Park in Shilin. Rides are cheap and paid per ride, and ideal for an 8-year-old. Check opening days .
  • Afternoon: Somewhere indoors and air-conditioned to escape the heat: National Taiwan Science Education Center or the Taipei Astronomical Museum, both nearby.
  • Evening: Shilin Night Market, just down the road.
Day 4 · Leofoo Village Theme Park & safari 🦒
  • Getting there: About 1–1.5 hours from Taipei. Options are a private driver or car charter (easiest with a child and luggage), or HSR to Hsinchu plus a shuttle bus .
  • All day: Leofoo Village Theme Park, which combines theme-park rides with a drive-through African safari area. Check the height limits on rides and whether there are animal-feeding sessions .
  • Evening: Stay the night at the on-site resort (option) instead of going back to Taipei.
Day 5 · Farm day: bottle-feed calves 🐄
  • Morning: Drive to Flying Cow Ranch (飛牛牧場) in Tongxiao, Miaoli. There's calf bottle-feeding, sheep and ducks, and fresh milk treats. The drive from Leofoo takes roughly 1–1.5 hours .
  • Afternoon: Drive back north (about 2 hours). It's a long day, so aim to be at the hotel by early evening.
  • Evening: An easy dinner near the hotel.
Day 6 · Xpark aquarium → fly home 🐧
  • Morning: Xpark aquarium next to HSR Taoyuan station, which has penguins, jellyfish and a big tank. It's close to the airport. Book timed tickets and check whether there is luggage storage .
  • Lunch: At Gloria Outlets next door.
  • Afternoon: Airport MRT or taxi to TPE, arriving 3 hours before the flight.

Swap-in options

  • Houtong Cat Village + Shifen (half or full day, by train on the Pingxi line). Friendly street cats in Houtong, then Shifen Waterfall and a sky lantern. Only pet the cats where it's allowed .
  • Yangmingshan, Qingtiangang: grassland with free-roaming water buffalo. Look from a distance only; don't touch or feed them.
  • Rainy-day backup: Taipei 101 observatory, museum days, or indoor kids' play centres.

ℹ️ Practical info

  • Weather: June is hot (often above 30 °C) and very humid, with afternoon thunderstorms. The plum-rain season is ending and typhoon season is starting, so keep the plan flexible.
  • Holiday: The Dragon Boat Festival (端午節) falls around 9 June 2027 . Expect a long weekend with crowds and higher prices, but you can also watch the boat races.
  • Entry / visa: This depends on your passports. Check the official rules, including whether an online arrival card is required .
  • Currency: New Taiwan Dollar (TWD). Night markets and small shops often take cash only.
  • Power plugs: Type A/B, 110 V. You may need an adapter.
  • Time zone: UTC+8, with no daylight saving.
  • Getting around: An EasyCard works on the MRT, buses, and in convenience stores. The MRT is clean and easy with kids.
