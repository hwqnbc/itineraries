## Research & options
- **Dates:** Decide whether to include the Dragon Boat Festival long weekend (around 9 June 2027, needs checking). It brings crowds, but the boat races are fun.
- **June or December?** Undecided; the dates on the trip page stay June for now. See the [Taiwan seasons guide](../../docs/countries/taiwan.md).

  | | June 2027 | December 2027 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Weather | 24–32 °C, very humid, plum rains until mid-June | 15–21 °C, often drizzly in Taipei |
  | Risks | Start of typhoon season; heat on zoo/theme-park days | Wet days: needs more indoor backups; shorter daylight |
  | Holidays | Dragon Boat Festival about 9 Jun {verify} | New Year's Eve crowds; flights dearer from about 20 Dec |
  | Kid extras | Water parks open | Strawberry picking (Dahu, Miaoli) {verify}; hot springs |
  | Plan changes if December | — | Swap Xpark/indoor options in on wet days; strawberry farm could replace or join the Day 5 farm; check which Leofoo areas are open in winter {verify} |
- **Days 4–5 transport:** A car charter is easier with an 8-year-old. HSR plus shuttle buses is cheaper but has more changes.
- **Leofoo:** Decide between staying one night at the resort and doing it as a day trip. Also check whether the water park is open in June.
- **Farm options:** Flying Cow Ranch is the first choice. Look for alternatives closer to Taipei if Days 4–5 feel too long.
- **Cats:** Houtong Cat Village can replace any day if we need a lighter one.
