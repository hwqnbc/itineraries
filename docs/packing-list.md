# Base packing list

Start from this list for every trip, then add trip-specific extras on the destination page under **Packing notes**.

## Documents & money (keep physical copies offline, not in this repo)
- [ ] Passports (check they're valid for at least 6 months after the trip)
- [ ] Visas / arrival cards, if required
- [ ] Travel insurance details
- [ ] Bank cards (tell the bank you're travelling) and some local cash
- [ ] Printed or offline copies of bookings

## Tech
- [ ] Phones and chargers
- [ ] Power bank (in carry-on only)
- [ ] Plug adapter(s)
- [ ] eSIM or SIM for data
- [ ] Headphones, and a tablet with offline shows for the child

## Health
- [ ] Regular medicines (in carry-on)
- [ ] Basic first-aid: plasters, antiseptic, painkillers or fever medicine (adult and child doses)
- [ ] Motion sickness remedy
- [ ] Sunscreen, insect repellent
- [ ] Hand sanitiser and wet wipes

## Clothes (per person)
- [ ] Enough outfits for the trip length ÷ 2 (plan to do laundry)
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes, plus sandals
- [ ] Light jacket
- [ ] Sleepwear
- [ ] Swimwear
- [ ] Hat and sunglasses

## For the child
- [ ] Small backpack with snacks and a water bottle
- [ ] Comfort toy
- [ ] Activity book or cards for the flight
- [ ] Spare change of clothes in the day bag (animal feeding gets messy)

## Day bag
- [ ] Water bottles
- [ ] Compact umbrella
- [ ] Tissues and wipes
- [ ] Small plastic bags
