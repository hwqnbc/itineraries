# Profile: default-family

The default group for trips unless a trip's `trip.md` names a different profile.

## Members
| Role | Age group | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Adult 1 | Adult, male | Loves good food; a nice café stop if it's on the way |
| Adult 2 | Adult, female | Enjoys shopping for clothes and accessories when it's nearby |
| Child | Boy, 8 (as of 2026) | Loves animals and theme parks |

## Interests
- **Animal encounters (top priority):** feeding and petting sessions at farms, petting zoos, aquariums with touch pools, and zoos with feeding times.
- **Theme parks:** rides suitable for an 8-year-old. Always check height limits.
- **Good food (Adult 1):** well-rated local food and cafés. Fit a café or a known good eatery into the day **when it's on the way or next to a planned stop**. Don't make detours for it.
- **Shopping (Adult 2):** clothes and accessories. Note malls, markets or shopping streets **next to or on the way to** a planned stop. Late afternoons and rainy spells suit shopping well, since the child can rest or play nearby.

## Pace & energy
- Moderate. At most **2 big activities a day**.
- A midday break (lunch plus somewhere indoors or back at the hotel) on hot days.
- Early dinners, with no late nights two days running.

## Must-dos
- At least one hands-on animal feeding or petting experience per trip.
- At least one theme park day where there is a good one.

## Deal-breakers / avoid
- Attractions where animal welfare is poor (e.g. riding elephants, animal shows that use performing wild animals).
- More than about 2.5 hours of driving in a day, unless it's a planned transfer day.

## Planning rules
- Put the most active day early in the trip, not the day after arriving.
- Always have an indoor backup for each outdoor day (weather).
- Keep long-transfer days to themselves; don't add major sights to them.
- **Food and shopping ride along, they don't lead.** For each day, check whether a good café or eatery, or a clothes/accessories shopping spot, is at or near that day's stops. If so, add a short `- **Food:** …` or `- **Shopping:** …` line to the day. Don't add extra travel for them, and don't count them as one of the 2 big activities.
