# Malaysia: seasons & holidays

Reusable guide for every trip to Malaysia. Weather notes are for the **west coast (KL, Melaka, Penang)** unless noted. The east coast and its islands have a different rainy season.

## Seasons at a glance
Malaysia is hot and humid all year (about 23–33 °C) {verify}. There are no four seasons; instead there are two monsoons and two wetter "inter-monsoon" spells.

| Period | Months | West coast (KL) | Watch out |
|--------|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| Northeast monsoon | Nov–Mar | Afternoon storms, fairly wet in Nov–Dec | **The east coast (Terengganu, Kelantan, Perhentian and Redang islands) gets heavy rain; many island resorts close** |
| Inter-monsoon | Apr–May | Wettest spell in KL, with big afternoon thunderstorms | Plan outdoor things for the morning |
| Southwest monsoon | Jun–Sep | Drier in KL; occasional haze from regional fires | Good months for the east-coast islands |
| Inter-monsoon | Oct–Nov | Very wet afternoons in KL | Flash floods on some roads |

Highlands (Genting, Cameron Highlands) are about 15–25 °C and misty all year {verify}.

## Public & school holidays (late 2026 – 2027)
Many dates follow the lunar or Islamic calendars and change each year {verify}. States add their own holidays.

## Friday prayers
Friday midday prayers affect opening hours across Malaysia {verify}:
- **When:** roughly **12:15pm–2:45pm** on Fridays (the exact time shifts a little through the year).
- **Government-run places** (museums, the National Planetarium, state attractions) often close, pause or skip shows then. The Planetarium stays open but has **no dome shows at 1pm and 2pm** on Fridays; the Sultan Alam Shah Museum (Shah Alam) closes 12:30–2:45pm.
- **Mosques** are closed to visitors until mid-afternoon (e.g. Putra Mosque: about 3–4pm and 5:30–6pm only).
- **Small Muslim-owned shops, food stalls and kopitiam** may close for a couple of hours, especially **outside the big malls and in smaller towns**. Malls, chains and Chinese or Indian eateries usually stay open.
- **Plan Fridays:** do government places in the **morning**, have lunch in a mall, and keep small towns, roadside stalls and mosque visits for other days or late afternoon.
- **Weekends differ by state:** most states (KL, Selangor, Johor, Melaka) have a Saturday–Sunday weekend, but **Kelantan, Terengganu and Kedah** have a **Friday–Saturday** weekend, so Friday is a rest day there {verify}.

## Seasonal things for kids
- **Year-round:** Sunway Lagoon, Genting SkyWorlds, Farm In The City, KL Bird Park, Aquaria KLCC, Legoland Malaysia (JB).
- **Fireflies** at Kuala Selangor: best on dark, dry nights, away from the full moon {verify}.
- **Festival lights:** Chinese New Year and Deepavali decorations in the malls; Christmas displays in December.
- **Rainy afternoons:** Petrosains, indoor theme parks and mall play areas.

## Basics
- **Currency:** Malaysian Ringgit (MYR). Cards, e-wallets and Touch 'n Go are widely used.
- **Power plugs:** Type G, 240 V, the same as Singapore.
- **Time zone:** UTC+8, the same as Singapore.
- **Entry:** Singapore passports are visa-free; check the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card rules {verify}.
- **Driving:** left-hand traffic. Tolls use Touch 'n Go or RFID. Singapore-registered cars need a VEP {verify}. KL rush hours are about 7–9:30am and 5–8pm.
- **Getting around KL without a car:** Grab, LRT/MRT and the monorail; the KTM ETS for intercity trips.
