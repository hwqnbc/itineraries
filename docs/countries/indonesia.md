# Indonesia (Bali): seasons & holidays

Reusable guide for trips to Indonesia, currently written for **Bali**. Other islands have similar seasons, but their holidays and customs differ.

## Seasons at a glance
Bali is tropical: warm all year, with a **dry season** and a **wet season** instead of four seasons.

| Season | Months | Weather | Good for / watch out |
|--------|--------|---------|----------------------|
| Dry | Apr–Oct | 24–31 °C, sunny, lower humidity | **Best time for kids' outdoor days** (safari, water parks, beaches). Busiest in Jul–Aug |
| Wet | Nov–Mar | 25–32 °C, humid, heavy afternoon/evening downpours | Mornings are often fine. Flooded roads slow down driving. Rougher sea on some beaches |

The highlands (Bedugul, Kintamani) are about 5–8 °C cooler all year {verify}.

## Public & school holidays (2027)
Balinese Hindu holidays follow the Balinese calendars, so the dates move every year {verify}.

| Date (2027) | Holiday | Effect on travel |
|-------------|---------|------------------|
| about March {verify} | **Nyepi, the Day of Silence** | **The whole island shuts for 24 hours:** the airport closes, and no one may leave their hotel. Don't plan arrivals or departures on this day. The night before has Ogoh-ogoh parades |
| Every 210 days {verify} | Galungan and Kuningan | Beautiful decorations (penjor). Some temples and shops close |
| 17 Aug | Independence Day | Local celebrations |
| Late Jun–mid Jul {verify} | Indonesian school holidays | Domestic crowds at parks |
| Late Jun–Jul, late Sep–early Oct, Dec–Jan {verify} | Australian school holidays | Bali is very busy with families; book parks and hotels early |
| Eid al-Fitr, about March 2027 {verify} | End of Ramadan (national holiday) | Domestic travel peak across Indonesia; Bali less affected |

## Seasonal things for kids
- **Turtle hatchling releases** at conservation centres: seasonal, often in the dry months {verify}.
- **Ogoh-ogoh parades** the evening before Nyepi: giant monster floats, very popular with children.
- **Kite season** in the dry months, especially Jul–Aug: huge traditional kites over Sanur and the rice fields.
- **Year-round:** Bali Zoo, Bali Safari Park, Waterbom and farm visits. In the wet season, plan the outdoor parts for the morning.

## Basics
- **Currency:** Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). Big numbers, so check the zeros when paying.
- **Power plugs:** Type C/F, 230 V.
- **Time zone:** Bali is UTC+8 (WITA). Jakarta is UTC+7.
- **Entry / visa:** visa on arrival or e-VOA for many passports; passports usually need 6 months' validity. There's also a separate **Bali tourist levy**, paid online or on arrival {verify}.
- **Getting around:** a car with driver, hired by the day, is the norm for families. Traffic in the south (Kuta, Seminyak, the airport) is slow at peak times.
- **Health:** drink bottled water; use mosquito repellent (dengue); strong sun all year.
- **Etiquette:** cover shoulders and knees at temples (sarongs are usually provided); don't step on the small offerings (canang) on the pavement.
