# Taiwan: seasons & holidays

Reusable guide for every trip to Taiwan. Weather figures are typical ranges for **northern Taiwan (Taipei)** unless noted. The south (Kaohsiung, Kenting) is warmer and drier in winter.

## Seasons at a glance
| Season | Months | Weather | Good for / watch out |
|--------|--------|---------|----------------------|
| Spring | Mar–May | 18–28 °C, mild; plum rains start in May | Pleasant sightseeing, flowers, fireflies |
| Summer | Jun–Aug | 30–35 °C, very humid, afternoon thunderstorms | Water parks and beaches. Heat is tiring for kids; typhoons from about July |
| Autumn | Sep–Nov | 22–30 °C, drying out | **Oct–Nov is the best weather of the year.** September can still have typhoons |
| Winter | Dec–Feb | 12–20 °C in Taipei, often grey and drizzly | Hot springs, strawberry picking. Pack layers and rain gear; the south is sunnier |

Temperature ranges are rough averages {verify}. Typhoons can close attractions and cancel flights, so keep a spare day in Jul–Sep trips.

## Public & school holidays (2027)

## Seasonal things for kids
- **Strawberry picking**, about Dec–Apr: Dahu, Miaoli {verify}. Hands-on, and fits a farm day.
- **Hot springs**, best Nov–Mar: Beitou (Taipei); family-friendly public baths and private rooms.
- **Cherry blossoms**, Feb–Mar: Yangmingshan.
- **Fireflies**, Apr–May: evening tours in the hills around Taipei {verify}.
- **Water parks and beaches**, Jun–Sep: many outdoor water parks only open in summer {verify}.
- **Year-round:** Taipei Zoo, Leofoo Village, Xpark and farm visits. Hot summer days need a midday indoor break; wet winter days need indoor backups.

## Basics
- **Currency:** New Taiwan Dollar (TWD); cash is still needed at night markets.
- **Power plugs:** Type A/B, 110 V.
- **Time zone:** UTC+8, no daylight saving.
- **Entry / visa:** depends on passport; check the official rules, including the online arrival card {verify}.
- **Transport card:** EasyCard (MRT, buses, convenience stores).
